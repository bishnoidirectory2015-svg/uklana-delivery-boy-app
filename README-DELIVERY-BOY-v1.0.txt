Uklana Delivery Boy App v1.0
Application ID: com.uklanafood.admin (already present in supplied Firebase google-services.json)
API: https://uklana.food/wp-json/ukf-delivery/v1
Flow: Boy 1 gets 3 alerts; Accept locks order; Forward sends to Boy 2; Boy 2 forwards to Boy 3. Server timeout also advances after 70 seconds. Accepted boy alone sees customer/order/payment details. COD cash total accumulates on Delivered. Reset keeps last 10 histories.
